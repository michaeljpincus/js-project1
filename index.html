Atlas of the Newest New Yorkers

This web map was created using data from The Newest New Yorkers: A Statistical Portrait of Foreign-born Residents and Their Role in Shaping the City's Population, a 2026 report by the NYC Department of City Planning. As neighborhoods are often informally defined, this map uses neighborhood boundaries as defined by the report. The findings of the report come from the 5-year 2019-2023 American Community Survey.
